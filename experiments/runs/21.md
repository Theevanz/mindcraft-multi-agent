## Verdict: STRATEGY issue — variable REGRESSED (identical signature ×2 bots)

Fly batching trades per-cell fly overhead for per-cell NAV timeouts: one flight lands the bot within reach of one cell
but strands it >4.5 from most others, and placeBlock's walk fallback cannot close the gap — a hovering bot (andy1 at
[12,-54,-540], y+2 above L4) has no walkable path, and L4 sits 3 above foot level (andy2 at y=-59), unclimbable without
pillaring under noDigNavigate. Every L4 cell burned a 30s nav timeout ("threw nav timeout (30s)" on all sampled fails),
stretching one pass to 8–9 min and freezing the score at 60.24%. The old per-cell fly wasn't just positioning — it was a
proximity guarantee (≤~3 blocks) for every placement, and batching deleted it. Early pace (L0–L3 in ~2 min) shows batching
works exactly where cells are foot-reachable, which is also where it saves the least.

## Observation

Reachability has two halves and the scaffold only owns one: flight gets the bot to the work area, but placement assumes
per-cell proximity that only per-cell flight provided. Any future pace work must preserve the invariant "no placement
attempt starts >4.5 from its cell" — e.g. proximity-guarded fly (fly iff the NEXT cell is out of reach) rather than
time-guarded fly (once per pass). L4 was always fly-dependent; batching just proved it by removing the dependency.
